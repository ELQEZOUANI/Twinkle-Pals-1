Dress up adorable avatars, play little adventures and make every outfit your own.

Step into a colorful world of friendship and imagination. Create lovely looks, help your pals through playful stories, and celebrate with stars, keepsakes and outfit gifts.

CREATE A LOOK YOU LOVE
Meet 12 original pals with their own styles and personalities. Mix hairstyles, hair colors, skin tones, outfits, shoes, hats, glasses and accessories. Try a flower crown, a bunny bag or a whole new look. Tap your pal to wave, or choose Surprise me for an unexpected outfit.

STYLE, PLAY AND CELEBRATE
Explore six story adventures, from Garden gathering and Beach besties to Cozy club, Superstar, Little explorer and Cat party. Follow outfit clues, help a friend and choose a keepsake for your adventure stamp book.

LITTLE ACTIVITIES, HAPPY DISCOVERIES
Find flowers, match ocean treasures, prepare snacks, follow dance sequences, explore a treasure trail and care for kittens. Choose between two story routes in each adventure and progress through three levels. Friendly hints and activities without timers let you play at your own pace.

COLLECT A LITTLE MAGIC
Earn adventure stars, friendship hearts and bonus sparkles. Discover six special wardrobe gifts, including Flower wings, a Mermaid bag, a Pearl bow, a Stardust dress, Explorer boots and a Kitten purse. More stars bring more styling surprises.

KEEP YOUR FAVORITE PALS
Save your favorite looks in My pals, give them names and return to style them again. Choose from six illustrated scenes and three poses, then save or share a picture of your creation.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Enjoy gentle character animations and playful sound effects, with sound and reduced-motion controls in Settings. Your saved pals and adventure progress stay on your device. No account is required.

FOR PARENTS
Luna, Milo and the Garden gathering adventure are available to start. Additional characters and adventures can be unlocked through optional rewarded ads. The app also includes ads that may appear at launch or after completed adventures. An internet connection is needed for ads and ad-based unlocks.

Dream it. Dress it. Make it yours.
